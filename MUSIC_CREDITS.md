# King Archer — Music Credits

All music in King Archer is **original**: the war-themed pieces (Defend + Battle layer, Boss, Raid) were composed for this game
(no existing melodies are used or quoted) and rendered offline from MIDI with sampled instruments, then mixed and mastered
(convolution hall reverb, EQ, glue compression, limiter, -16 LUFS).
